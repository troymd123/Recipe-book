Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Version 2026-11-08

Recipe Registry (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is operated by an individual in Connecticut doing business as Recipe Registry. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. You can reach us at support@alltroysfault.com, or from Support in the app’s menu.

1. What we collect

Your account. Your email address and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and stored in scrambled (hashed) form; we never see it. When you create an account we also record which version of these documents you agreed to, and when.

Date of birth (optional). If you add one, it is kept private and is used only to decide whether you may see and share 21+ recipes (recipes with alcohol). It can be set once. If you skip it, you can instead confirm you are over 21 when you turn 21+ recipes on; we record that confirmation and the time.

Your content. Recipes you save (names, ingredients, directions, notes, links, tags, ratings and comments), meal plans, shopping lists, pantry items, favorites, and the family books you create or join.

What you share. Recipes you share with family books are visible to the members of those books. Recipes you share to the recipe feed are visible to everyone who uses the app, together with the display name you choose. Your star ratings are added into each recipe’s average and are not shown to others one by one.

Messages and reports. Support messages you send, and reports you file about recipes (the category and your description).

Usage and safety records. Daily counters for features with limits (for example AI extractions), when you last used limited features, and moderation records such as recipes removed after reports and any resulting account restrictions.

Technical information. Our hosting and security providers receive technical data such as your IP address and device or browser details when you use the app. If you see ads, our advertising partners may also collect device identifiers, your IP address and approximate location, and information about the ads you view or interact with (see “Ads”). The app also stores your sign-in session and preferences on your device (see “Cookies and local storage”).

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: save and sync your recipes, plans and lists, and show shared recipes to the people you chose.
  • To power AI features (reading recipes from links, photos, text and audio; estimating nutrition; suggesting recipes and swaps; building shopping lists).
  • To keep the app safe: screening shared recipes and names for content that is not allowed, handling reports, applying limits, preventing abuse, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To respond to support requests and send messages you ask for, such as a password reset.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for money. The app shows ads to free accounts, which involves advertising partners as described in “Ads” below.

3. AI and service providers

We use other companies to run the app. They process information on our behalf:

  • Anthropic (Claude): receives the text, links, photos and other content you submit to AI features, recipe text when it is screened before being shared, and ingredient lists for suggestions. For recipe ideas from the web it may also search the web.
  • Cloudflare: hosts our server code, runs the security check at sign-up and sign-in, and provides speech-to-text for voice recipes (the audio you record is sent for transcription).
  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service, where your account and content are stored.
  • GitHub: hosts the app’s web pages.
  • Resend: sends emails such as password resets and support replies.
  • Advertising partners ([AD PARTNERS, e.g. Google Ad Manager]): provide and measure the ads shown to free accounts.

We send these providers only what is needed for the feature you use. We do not keep the original photos or audio you submit after they have been processed. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

4. Ads

Free accounts see ads. These may include banner ads and rewarded video ads, which are short videos you can choose to watch to unlock a free use of some features. Premium plans may reduce or remove ads.

Ads are provided by advertising partners. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to help prevent fraud, they may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies, and may show personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your settings and where you live. When you watch a rewarded video, we learn that you finished it so we can give you the use you earned.

We do not share your recipes, email address or date of birth with advertising partners. Where choices are offered (for example a consent screen, or ad settings on your device or with the ad partner), you can use them to limit personalized ads. Advertising partners have their own privacy policies.

5. Who can see what

  • Only you can see your private recipes, plans, lists, pantry, date of birth and ratings.
  • Members of a family book can see the recipes shared with that book and your display name in it.
  • Everyone using the app can see what you share to the recipe feed.
  • Our administrators can see content and records needed to provide support, review reports and keep the app safe.
  • Recipes with alcohol are labeled 21+ and are shown only to people confirmed to be 21 or older.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account exists. You can export your recipes, deactivate your account (which hides what you shared and pauses emails while keeping your data), or permanently delete it, all from Settings. Deleting removes your recipes, plans, lists, pantry, shares, ratings, support messages and account. Copies of your shared recipes that other people saved to their own collections stay with them. Some information may remain for a limited time in backups or provider logs before it is removed, and we may keep limited records where the law requires it or to prevent abuse.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your recipes and settings in the app, export them, and deactivate or delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as asking what we hold about you, asking us to correct or delete it, or asking for a copy. Contact us at support@alltroysfault.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

The app is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you add a date of birth showing you are under 13, you cannot use the app. If we learn that we have information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you are a parent and believe your child has an account, please contact us.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, access controls, and a sign-in service that stores passwords in hashed form. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Where information is processed

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

11. Cookies and local storage

The app stores a sign-in session and your preferences (for example your plan view, whether 21+ recipes are on, and a name you chose for sharing) in your browser’s local storage so it can work and remember you. Our advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies, as described in “Ads”. You can clear the data in your browser, which will sign you out, and manage ad-related cookies through your browser or device settings and, where offered, our consent choices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make an important change we will say so in the app, and the “Last updated” date above will change. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact
